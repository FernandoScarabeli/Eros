# Code of Conduct

We want Eros to be a place where people can build, ask questions and disagree
without harassment or humiliation.

- Discuss ideas and code with respect; keep criticism specific and constructive.
- Welcome different backgrounds, identities and levels of experience.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or deliberately intimidate people.
- Do not publish someone else's private information without permission.
- Respect boundaries and stop unwanted contact when asked.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions,
restrict participation or ban participants when necessary. Decisions should be
proportionate, consider context and avoid exposing reporters' private information.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers. A verified private contact channel
must be configured and documented before public contributions open; see the
[publication checklist](docs/publishing.md). Do not include private reports in a
public issue or use this policy to demand public disclosure of a reporter.
